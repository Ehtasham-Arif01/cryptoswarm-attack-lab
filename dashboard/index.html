Birthday attack: forging a signed contract

In a room of just 23 people, two share a birthday with 50% probability. The same math breaks hashes: an 34-bit hash has 17.2 G values, but a collision is expected after only about 146 k hashes (≈ √N).

1. Bob writes the fair contract ($100) and a fraud ($100,000) in 224 wordings each.
2. The swarm hashes both sets until a fair and a fraud wording have the same hash.
3. Alice signs the fair one. Her signature (made on the hash) also verifies on the fraud.

Cracking a leaked password table

A website's user table leaked: 12 usernames and their stored password hashes. The swarm tries common words × mangling rules (Dragon, sh4d0w, monkey2019, pepper123!…). How the site stored the passwords decides how long that takes.
